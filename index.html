About

I'm a cybercrime consultant based in Bengaluru, working on the parts of the internet that are easiest to get wrong — scam infrastructure, fraudulent personas, and the trail they leave behind. Most of my work is open-source intelligence: investigating cybercrime, triaging incidents for individuals and startups, and working out whether the thing in front of someone is real.

Writing is the other half of it. I publish long-form infosec notes on Medium, because complex ideas are worth the effort of making them readable.

Away from the terminal I volunteer with BSides Bangalore as a core team member, follow politics closely, and keep programming around as a hobby rather than only a job.
